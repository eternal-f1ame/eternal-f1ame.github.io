these used and empty pens
some with caps and caps amiss

oh, there are packets on the floor
chocolates, noodles, and chips


and whose bottle is that one
I recognize it, and I don’t

sitting like a gangsta,
amidst the cups and spoons that I stole


slippers a piece each from different pairs
one with the kit-kat wrapper I bought last week

these posters on the walls 
and a messy “wardrobe to be.”


the laptop plugged in on the table
with hundreds of tabs, new and old

an unwashed kettle of delicacies 
lobby’s all-purpose stove


why are oregano packets not with pizza
decorated with my facewash and sunscreen

t-shirts with my name
every corner that I  see


these tangled wires and the boards
which may zap me again 

that one earpiece from a pair
which might have been used in last May


there won't be gatherings anymore 
no more UNO games to be played.

what’s this strange feeling of 
“I wish I could have stayed.”


these used and empty pens
so many stories they have inked

room 162 B1 I will remember you
for the many lives that you have seen.
