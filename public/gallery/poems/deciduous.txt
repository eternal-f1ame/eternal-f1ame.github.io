Another moment gone, another arrives,
Deciduous days of these vibrant lives,
So much it changes, this notorious time.


Mind so cold and heart in bits
Still, do we treasure this moment as a gift?
New beginnings, new puzzling ways
Deciduous lives in vibrant days 


Fluid time, my love a jar
Battered cap, broken apart
Deciduous joys fleeting by
Beauty, seen only through vibrant eyes 


A minute gone is another to live
Dearth the brood, the hour's bliss
Someday you will get to live and thrive,
Last, deciduous are days and vibrant all lives

    ~ Aaditya Baranwal
