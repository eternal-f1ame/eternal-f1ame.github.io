*what’s changed?*

different time, old places,
same homes, different faces.

faint smell, metallic rust
cold sweat and evening breeze,
stray dogs, all hush
unending barks on the lease.

what’s changed?

engines dying, faint sound
distant melody, car honk

jay walking, skid walks
ritual? much adorned.

ice-creams, bought all,
happiness? 2014 fall.

what’s changed?

playmates, stolen light
illusion, seems nice.

lucrative 
doorbells, diverted eyes
choked
favourite names, no replies

what changed?

noise, smoke, refreshing wind
the holy, the sham, the jungle and concrete.
leckered flavours, ghats and temples, gory deaths, still here to see.

what changed?

planned escape, plotted flee, feel embraced?
run walk, totter crawl, halted pace?

changed

new times, new places,
different homes, unknown faces.


    ~ Aaditya Baranwal
