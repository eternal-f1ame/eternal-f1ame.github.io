bumpy hot and loud
potholes vehicles and crowd
all traffic cowed

deafened, ears fought
crank! run! run! someone shouts
all honks? now naught

dusty, wheres the pass
GODS! what has come to pass
all gone, who didn't pass

cars are now flat junk
people in them are now shrunk
all misery, clunk

do I turn to see
frozen, I now live to glee
none, I run to flee
    ~ Aaditya Baranwal
