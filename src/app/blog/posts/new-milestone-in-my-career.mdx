## PhD Candidate: University of Central Florida

New chapter. I'm joining **The University of Central Florida** this fall as a **Doctoral Candidate (PhD Computer Science) with an AII Fellowship**. Joint supervision under **Dr. Yogesh Rawat and Dr. Shruti Vyas**.

---

### The Moment It Hit

January 26th. Emails from **Bosch, Microsoft, and UCF** arrived within the hour. It felt like every effort converged, a surreal moment where a distant dream became real.

---

### The Path Taken

No straight line here. Challenges defined the journey: **ABU Robocon 2022**, the **Michelin Mobility Innovation Challenge 2022** with its **Vision-based semi-autonomous Mining Robot**, founding **RAID** at IIT Jodhpur. Each obstacle was a build.

---

### The Collective Push

I didn't get here alone. Mentors, colleagues, friends, and family – their push, guidance, and tolerance for my quirks were invaluable. This milestone reflects their influence as much as my own drive.

And to **Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur** – thanks for the opportunities, the challenges. Made me someone I'm proud to be.
