Developed a robust GAN-generated image detection method using an ensemble of CNN and ViT gradients with depthwise convolutions. Achieved state-of-the-art results: cross-model accuracy > 75%, cross-category accuracy > 89%, and overall accuracy of 97.75%, demonstrating resilience against image and frequency space artifacts.
