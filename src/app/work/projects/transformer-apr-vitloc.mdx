Achieved SOTA Absolute Pose Regression with two novel approaches (1.78m/3.37◦ & 2.02m/3.18◦ ).

ThreeFormer employs a multi-stream approach with dedicated position, orientation, and backbone transformers to emphasize different visual cues, using self-attention to aggregate pose-relevant features. FeatFormer integrates 3D feature matching. ViT backbone to extract dense visual features projected into a 3D feature volume via NeRF.
